# Internal Linking Implementation Guide

A practical guide to planning, implementing, auditing, and maintaining useful internal links across a website.

> Scope: Internal linking should help users navigate related information and help search systems discover and understand website relationships. Internal links do not guarantee rankings, indexing, or visibility in search or AI systems.

---

## 1. Understand the Purpose of Internal Links

An internal link connects one page on a website to another page on the same website.

Useful internal links can help:

- Users discover related information
- Users navigate between stages of a task
- Search systems discover URLs
- Search systems understand relationships between pages
- Websites organize related topics
- Important pages receive appropriate internal references

Internal linking should primarily serve a useful navigation or information purpose.

---
