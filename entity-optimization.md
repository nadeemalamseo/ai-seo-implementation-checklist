# Entity Optimization Implementation Guide

A practical guide to making important people, organizations, products, services, topics, and other entities clear, consistent, and understandable across a website.

> Scope: Entity optimization is about accurately describing real entities and their relationships. It does not guarantee rankings, knowledge-panel inclusion, AI citations, or visibility in any particular search system.

---

## 1. Understand What an Entity Is

An entity is a distinct person, organization, place, product, service, concept, or other identifiable thing.

Examples include:

- Person
- Organization
- Company
- Brand
- Product
- Service
- Local business
- Website
- Publication
- Topic
- Place

Entity optimization begins with identifying what the website is actually about.

Do not create artificial entities or relationships simply to influence search systems.

---

## 2. Identify the Primary Entity

Start by determining the main entity associated with each important page.

Examples:

```text
Company homepage
└── Primary entity: Organization

Author profile
└── Primary entity: Person

Product page
└── Primary entity: Product

Service page
└── Primary entity: Service

Local business page
└── Primary entity: LocalBusiness

Article
└── Primary entity: Article
    └── About: Topic or subject
