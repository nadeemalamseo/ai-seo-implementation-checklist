# Content Optimization Implementation Guide

A practical guide to creating, improving, evaluating, and maintaining useful search-focused content.

> Scope: Content optimization should improve usefulness, clarity, accuracy, and accessibility for the intended audience. These practices do not guarantee rankings, traffic, indexing, citations, or visibility in any particular search or AI system.

---

## 1. Start With the User's Need

Before creating content, identify what the user is actually trying to accomplish.

Ask:

- What problem are they trying to solve?
- What question are they asking?
- What information do they need?
- What action might they need to take?
- What level of knowledge do they have?
- What would make the page genuinely useful?

The primary objective should be solving the user's information need.

A keyword alone is not a sufficient content strategy.

---

## 2. Identify Search Intent

Determine the likely intent behind the topic.

Common intent categories include:

- Informational
- Navigational
- Commercial investigation
- Transactional
- Local
- Support or troubleshooting

A single query can have multiple possible intents.

Review the actual wording, context, and search results when determining the likely intent.

---

## 3. Define the Primary Audience

Document who the content is intended for.

For example:

```text
Topic: Technical SEO Audit

Primary audience:
Website owners and SEO practitioners

Knowledge level:
Beginner to intermediate

Primary need:
Understand what to check and how to identify technical problems

Desired outcome:
Complete a practical technical SEO review
