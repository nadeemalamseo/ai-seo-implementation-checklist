# SEO Measurement & Monitoring Implementation Guide

A practical guide to measuring technical SEO, content, AI-search readiness, website visibility, and meaningful performance changes.

> Scope: Measurement should help identify changes, problems, and opportunities. Metrics do not prove causation by themselves, and SEO or AI-search measurements cannot guarantee rankings, citations, traffic, or conversions.

---

## 1. Start With Measurement Goals

Before collecting metrics, define what you are trying to understand.

Common objectives include:

- Search visibility
- Organic traffic
- Indexing
- Crawl and technical health
- Content performance
- Internal-linking changes
- Conversion activity
- Product performance
- Lead generation
- Brand searches
- Referral traffic
- AI-search observations

A metric is useful when it helps answer a specific question.

Do not collect numbers simply because a tool provides them.

---

## 2. Establish a Baseline

Before making significant changes, record the current state.

A baseline may include:

- Date
- Important URLs
- Indexed-page observations
- Organic clicks
- Organic impressions
- Search queries
- Average position
- Organic sessions
- Conversions
- Revenue where applicable
- Referral traffic
- Technical errors
- Crawl observations
- Content inventory
- Internal-linking state
